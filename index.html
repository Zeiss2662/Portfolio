About

Background

I am an undergraduate student at Daffodil International University, studying Information and Communication Engineering. My interest in software engineering grew from building practical tools and from seeing how much everyday systems depend on good design.

Alongside my studies, I have explored emerging technologies including blockchain, and I am developing an interest in artificial intelligence and data science, particularly for climate and environmental research. My first accepted research abstract applies night-time light data to urban inequality in South Asian cities.

I have worked in client relations, customer support and team leadership, and I take part in community and university activities as a mentor, volunteer and organizing committee member.
